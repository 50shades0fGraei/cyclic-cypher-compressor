## 1. THE AUTOMATED REVENUE PIPELINE (Zero-Touch Money Flow)
Before hiring, the system must enforce payments mathematically. The `Lujan_SaaS_API.py` using **Stripe Connect** handles this:
*   **The Lock:** The Cypher Engine will not process client data unless the client has a valid, funded API Key.
*   **The Split:** Stripe automatically captures the 80% Symbiotic Cypher Fee.
*   **The Payout:** Stripe is configured to automatically sweep those funds into your primary business bank account every 48 hours. **No manual invoicing required.**
